How It Works

Three simple steps to finding a roommate who matches your vibe and college budget.

1

Create Your Profile

Register with your college details, monthly budget limit, diet habits (Veg/Non-Veg), and study routine (Early Bird or Night Owl).

2

Filter & Compare

Browse student profiles across campus zones. Filter by budget range, dietary preferences, cleanliness, and lifestyle habits.

3

Connect & Move In

Send roommate requests, review mutual interests, verify compatibility, and secure your shared flat before the semester starts.
